Known issues — Minecraft 26.2 port (mc/26.2)
=================================================
These are pre-existing upstream issues or cosmetic differences,
not regressions introduced by this port. Logged for awareness
and potential future fixes.

1. Shears tool-indicator logic has a pre-existing bug
   (stuck wrong-tool indicator on cobweb, missing icon on leaves/wool).
   Confirmed present in stock WTHIT 26.1 as well — not introduced
   by this port.

2. Tab styling uses a custom flat-color look instead of stock
   vanilla dark/white-border tabs. This was never a correct
   reimplementation of the original (which used vanilla's default
   TabButton rendering), just a plausible-looking substitute that
   happened to compile. Candidate for a future enhancements pass
   to make it vanilla-consistent.
